About this property

Step into this beautifully designed duplex apartment, perfectly positioned in the heart of Nine Elms. Blending sleek contemporary style with homely comfort, this spacious two-bedroom property offers the ideal base for families, business travellers, contractors, and groups visiting London.

From the moment you arrive, you're welcomed by a bright open-plan layout, elegant décor, and generous natural light flowing across two thoughtfully arranged levels — offering privacy, comfort, and ample space for everyone to relax and unwind.

The spacious living area is designed with comfort and functionality in mind, making it easy to socialise, work remotely, or simply wind down after a day in the city. Two full bathrooms add everyday convenience and privacy, particularly appreciated by larger groups and business travellers.

Exceptionally well connected, Nine Elms Underground Station is just 2–3 minutes' walk away, Vauxhall Station around 5–10 minutes on foot, and local bus stops within metres of the door — putting the whole of London within easy reach. Whether you're staying for a few nights or several months, this stylish duplex delivers space, comfort, and unbeatable connectivity in one of London's most exciting neighbourhoods.
