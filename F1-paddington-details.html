About this property

Welcome to F1 – Pad, a stylish and thoughtfully designed 1-bedroom apartment, perfect for those seeking simplicity without compromising comfort.

The elegant living room is beautifully furnished, spotlessly clean, and filled with natural light—complete with cosy sofas for evening relaxation. The fully equipped kitchen includes a hob, oven, grill, fridge/freezer, dishwasher, kettle, and all essential cookware to make home-style meals with ease.

Guests benefit from fast Wi-Fi, a Smart TV, central heating, and seamless self check-in. Whether you're in London for business or leisure, this flat offers a welcoming, well-connected base close to the heart of the city.

A light-filled, minimalist 1-bedroom apartment offering comfort, style, and convenience for solo travellers or couples.
