Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about arriving, access, and flexibility at Bliss Living.

What are your check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 15:00, and check-out is by 11:00.

Early check-in or late check-out is available for £15 per hour (subject to availability). Please contact us in advance to arrange.

How does check-in work?

Most stays use self-service check-in via a lockbox or smart lock. Access details are sent to you via WhatsApp before arrival.

Can I cancel or change my booking?

Yes — policies depend on your booking type. Please check your confirmation email or contact us and we'll be happy to help.

Do I need to provide ID at check-in?

Yes, a valid ID with proof of address is required. You may also be asked to show your booking confirmation.

Can I store luggage before check-in or after check-out?

Yes, luggage drop-off is available for £25 between 11:00–15:00 (subject to availability). Please contact us in advance to arrange.
