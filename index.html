Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about staying with Bliss Living.

What is included in the price?

All our apartments are fully inclusive. Your rate covers rent, electricity, gas, water, council tax, and superfast broadband. No surprise bills — ever.

What is the minimum stay?

Our minimum stay is one week. We also offer longer-term monthly rates, which are available on request and offer better value for extended stays.

Are the apartments suitable for corporate stays?

Absolutely. Many of our guests are professionals on long-term projects or corporate relocations. We can provide invoices, arrange flexible terms, and accommodate group bookings across multiple apartments.

How do I book or enquire?

Simply fill in our enquiry form and we’ll get back to you within a few hours. You can also reach us via WhatsApp or phone. We’re a small, dedicated team — you’ll always speak to a real person.

Is linen and housekeeping provided?

Yes. All apartments are made up with fresh, high-quality linen before your arrival. We can also arrange regular housekeeping during your stay on request.

Can I extend my stay once I’ve moved in?

In most cases, yes. We try to accommodate extensions wherever possible. Just let us know as soon as you can and we’ll do our best to make it work.

Still have questions? Drop us an enquiry or chat with us on WhatsApp.
